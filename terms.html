Fancy Car — Terms of Use

Effective: November 15, 2026 · Anni Yang / Venturebnb LLC · Contact: info@annizon.com

WHAT FANCY CAR IS

Fancy Car is a record-keeping and information app: a lifetime file for your cars, plain-language explanations of fault codes and dashboard warning lights, maintenance reminders, and free export of your history.

WHAT IT IS NOT

Fancy Car does not connect to your car in this version. It does not scan, diagnose, or read your vehicle, it cannot clear codes, and it never states that a car is safe to drive. Explanations describe what a code or light generally means and list possible causes; they are educational information, not a diagnosis of your specific car. Only a mechanic's in-person inspection can diagnose your car. For brakes, airbags, steering, tires, fuel leaks, overheating, or loss of power, treat the matter as safety-critical and have the car inspected promptly.

YOUR RECORDS ARE YOURS

Records you create are backed up to your account automatically. Export is free forever in open formats. If you delete your account, your data is deleted as described in the Privacy Policy. Cars shared with family members must be transferred or deleted before the owner's account can close, so a family's record never vanishes because one account closed.

IDENTIFICATION ACCURACY

Photo identification returns a make, model, generation and a year RANGE, with a stated confidence; it can be wrong, and you can correct it in one tap before saving. The exact year, trim and engine come from a VIN decode (NHTSA vPIC) or your own documents. Fair-price figures are estimates shown as ranges with their basis — they are not quotes, and a shop's actual price depends on your car and your area.

PRICES

This version of Fancy Car is free. If paid features are introduced later, the price will be printed in plain sight before you pay anything, no trial will start without your explicit tap, and the records, reminders, backup and export described above will remain free.

ACCEPTABLE USE

Do not use Fancy Car to look up vehicles you have no connection to, and never enter another person's license plate — the app does not support plate lookups at all. You are responsible for the accuracy of records you keep.

LIABILITY

The app is provided as-is. To the extent permitted by law, we are not liable for decisions made using its explanations, estimates, or reminders. When in doubt about a safety matter, stop and get the car inspected.
