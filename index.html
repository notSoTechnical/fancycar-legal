What Fancy Car does

Fancy Car keeps a lifetime file for each of your cars: build the file from a photo of the car or its VIN, look up dashboard fault codes in plain language with fair-price ranges and the basis for each range, and turn maintenance reminders into service records with one tap. Family members can be added as owner, manager, or viewer.

Common questions

Can Fancy Car tell me whether my car is safe to drive?
No. Fancy Car explains what a fault code means and what repairs typically cost; it never gives a safe-to-drive verdict. If a warning light suggests a safety risk, have the car inspected by a qualified mechanic before driving it.

Why does a photo of my car only give a year range?
An exterior photo can identify the make, model, and generation, but not the exact model year. A photo of the VIN plate gives the exact year and trim, decoded with the free NHTSA vPIC database.

Does exporting my records cost anything?
No. Your full history export (PDF, CSV, JSON) is free in the app — Vehicle → Export — and stays free forever.

How do I delete my account?
In the app: Settings → Delete my account. Full instructions are on the account deletion page.
