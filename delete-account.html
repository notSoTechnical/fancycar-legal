Delete your Fancy Car account

Fancy Car is made by Anni Yang / Venturebnb LLC. Support: info@annizon.com.

Delete it in the app (fastest)

Open Fancy Car → Settings → Delete my account. Deletion is immediate and removes your profile, your vehicles, service records, reminders, saved explanations, and the documents and invoice photos stored with them.

One guard: if a car you own is shared with family members, the app first asks you to transfer ownership or remove the members, so a family's record never vanishes because one account closed. Cars where you are only a member are simply unlinked from your account.

No app access?

Email info@annizon.com from the address on your account with the subject "Delete my Fancy Car account". We delete the account by hand and confirm by email within 2 business days.

Before you delete

Your full history export (PDF, CSV, JSON) is free in the app — Vehicle → Export — and stays free forever. Export first if you want to keep the record; after deletion it cannot be recovered.
