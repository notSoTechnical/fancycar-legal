Fancy Car — Privacy Policy

Effective: November 15, 2026 · Anni Yang / Venturebnb LLC · Contact: info@annizon.com

Fancy Car keeps the lifetime file for your family's cars: identity, service records, reminders, and plain-language explanations of fault codes and warning lights. This policy says exactly what is stored, what is shared, with whom, and when.

YOUR ACCOUNT

Sign-in is an email link (no password), provided by Supabase, our database and sign-in provider. Your account holds your email address and your language preference. Your car files belong to this account, which is why an account exists at all: so the record survives a lost phone and can be shared with family you invite.

WHAT WE STORE

Your vehicles (including the VIN when you decode one), service and repair records, invoice and vehicle photos you add, maintenance reminders, family invitations and roles, the explanation lookups you save to a car's file, and a count of your AI identification calls (so the monthly allowance can be enforced fairly).

IDENTIFICATION AND DECODING

VIN decoding uses NHTSA vPIC, the US government's public vehicle database — the VIN you enter is sent to NHTSA (or our server, which asks NHTSA) and the decoded facts are stored in your file. Photo identification runs on our server with a third-party AI vision provider: the photo you submit is sent to that provider to be identified, and the result (never the provider's copy) is stored in your file. We never look up license plates.

FAMILY SHARING

When you invite a family member to a car, they can see and (as a manager) edit that car's file according to the role you chose. We show invited members your car's data because you asked us to — that is the feature. We do not share your data with anyone else for their own purposes.

YOUR DATA IS NEVER SOLD

There is no sale of data, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking SDK. Your data goes only to the providers that run the app — Supabase (database, sign-in, photo storage), NHTSA (VIN decoding), and the AI vision provider (photos you submit for identification) — and only what each needs to do its work.

EXPORT AND DELETION

Your full history export (PDF, CSV, JSON) is free, forever, inside the app. Deleting your account (Settings → Delete my account) removes your files, records and photos from our servers; if you no longer have the app, email info@annizon.com from your account email and we will delete it by hand and confirm.

QUESTIONS

Contact info@annizon.com. A human answers within 2 business days.
